# AI Guitar Lab

A free guitar practice app that runs in your browser. It covers theory, technique, ear training and four style paths (blues, rock, acoustic and jazz), all built around seeing the whole neck.

**Built with AI.** AI Guitar Lab was built entirely with AI (Claude, by Anthropic). Every line of code and every lesson was written by AI.

## What's inside

- **Home**: a daily whole-neck challenge, a roadmap from intermediate to advanced, and a 28-day course.
- **Practice**: pick-up-and-play sessions, a finger technique ladder, a check-up, ear training, neck-note drills and public-domain songs with tab and sound.
- **Theory**: six lessons, keys and the circle of fifths, CAGED shapes, triads, guide tones, intervals and a scale and mode explorer.
- **Styles**: blues, rock, acoustic and jazz lessons. Every lick can be moved up or down the neck.
- **Tools**: a jam station with drums, bass and chords in five styles, a tab builder, a metronome and a practice plan.

## How to open it

- **Online:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repository.
- **On your own computer:** download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. You don't need to install anything or run a build step.

Sound uses the Web Audio API, so tap or click once on the page to let the browser play audio.

## Your data

There are no accounts, no tracking and no server. Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. If you clear your browser data, your progress is cleared with it. The only outside request is for web fonts from Google Fonts.
